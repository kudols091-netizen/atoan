# Refund & Return Policy

**Last updated: July 31, 2026**

We want you to love what we make for you. If something is not right, here is exactly where you stand.

## Return Window

You have **30 days** from the date you receive your order to request a return. To be eligible, the item must be **unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging**.

## Handmade Means Small Differences

Every piece is crocheted by hand, one at a time. Stitch tension, facial expression, and colour placement vary slightly from one piece to the next, and yarn dye lots are never identical. These differences are the mark of handmade work and are **not** treated as defects.

Genuine faults — unravelling seams, loose safety eyes, a wrong item, or damage in transit — are always covered. Email us a photo and we will replace or refund it.

## Made-From-Your-Photo Orders

Pieces crocheted from a customer's own photo are made for one person and cannot be resold, so they **cannot be returned for a change of mind**. They are still fully covered if the piece arrives faulty, damaged, or clearly does not match what was agreed.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- Made-from-your-photo and other personalised pieces, unless faulty.
- Items marked final sale or clearance.
- Items that have been used, washed, altered, or damaged after delivery.
- Gift cards.

## Cancelling an Order

Because we start crafting soon after an order comes in, you can cancel for a **full refund within 24 hours** of ordering. After that, work is already underway and we can only cancel if your piece has not yet been started — email us and we will tell you honestly.

## How to Start a Return

1. Email us with your order number and what you would like to return.
2. We review the request and send return instructions within **1–2 business days**.
3. Ship the item back using the instructions provided.

Please do not send anything back before you have return instructions from us.

## Return Shipping Costs

Customers cover return shipping unless the item is faulty, damaged, or incorrect — in which case we cover it.

## Refunds

Once your return arrives and passes inspection, we issue a refund to the original payment method within **5–10 business days**. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is our fault. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to show the refund.
