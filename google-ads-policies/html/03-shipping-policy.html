Thank you for shopping with Luminouswand. Below is everything you need to know about how and when your order will arrive.

1. Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking once your order ships.

2. Shipping Times & Rates

Destination Estimated Delivery Shipping Cost
United States 7–15 business days Free over $49, otherwise $4.99
United Kingdom 7–15 business days Free over $49, otherwise $4.99
Canada 7–15 business days Free over $49, otherwise $4.99

Free shipping applies to US, UK, and Canada orders over $49 USD.

Estimated delivery times are in addition to the processing time above and are estimates, not guarantees. Delays may occur due to carrier issues, customs, weather, or peak seasons.

3. Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Please allow up to 48 hours for tracking to update after you receive it.

4. Customs, Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to import duties and taxes charged by the destination country. These are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the product price or shipping cost.

5. Incorrect Address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer. If you notice an error, contact us at info@luminouswand.com as soon as possible — we can only update the address before the order ships.

6. Lost or Delayed Packages

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated timeframe, please contact us at info@luminouswand.com and we'll work with the carrier to locate it.
